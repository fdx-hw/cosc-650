Sampling Explorer
Built and ran the temperature explorer (temp 0.5, 1.0, 2.0), and, per review feedback, added a top-k explorer (k = 5, 20, 50) so top_k is now actually called and charted, not just defined.
Top token probability (temperature panel): temp 0.5 = 0.933, temp 1.0 = 0.276, temp 2.0 = 0.010 — confirms higher temperature flattens the distribution and lowers confidence in the top choice.
Top token probability (top-k panel): top-k 5 = 0.704, top-k 20 = 0.526, top-k 50 = 0.449 — this decrease isn't the model getting less confident. The raw top-token probability doesn't change with k; it's renormalization: the same fixed numerator (top token's raw probability) gets divided by a progressively larger captured probability mass as k grows, so the reported "confidence" mechanically shrinks even though nothing about the model's certainty changed.
 
Evaluating Three Settings
temp 1e-6, top-p 1.0: entropy -0.000, max prob 1.000, nucleus keeps 1 token — near-greedy collapse, as predicted.
temp 0.7, top-k 50: entropy 1.810, max prob 0.710, entropy after top-50 filter 1.309 — expected the cutoff to barely matter given 71% confidence on the top token; instead entropy dropped meaningfully, showing a real chunk of uncertainty was coming from the long tail beyond rank 50, not close runners-up.
temp 1.3, top-p 0.9: entropy 7.480, max prob 0.084, nucleus keeps 4,982 tokens — matched prediction (flatter distribution, larger nucleus needed).
temp 0.7, top-k 50 + top-p 0.9: entropy 1.810, max prob 0.710, top-p keeps only 7 of the top-50 tokens (entropy 0.763) — shows the two filters compounding: even within the top-50 set, most probability sits with a handful of tokens.
 
Failure Cases
(a) top-p 1.0 is a mathematical no-op: even with a peaked distribution (71% on one token), it keeps the full vocabulary, since cumulative probability across all tokens always equals 1.0 by definition. Mitigation: use top-p meaningfully below 1.0 (e.g. 0.9) for actual filtering.
(b) "Negative" entropy from floating-point rounding: at temp 1e-6, entropy printed as -0.000, despite entropy being mathematically guaranteed non-negative. Caused by a probability rounding to ~0.9999999999 instead of exactly 1.0. Mitigation: clip to zero or use a small tolerance check before displaying.
